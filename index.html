Built for smart payment recovery decisions

The platform ranks high-value recovery opportunities, respects budget limits, and makes every action explainable for operations teams and finance leaders.

A

Action scoring

Estimate which recovery strategy is most likely to work based on customer behavior, transaction risk, and expected return.

P

Policy controls

Ensure only valid, safe, and compliant interventions reach customers. Business rules stay in front of model output.

B

Budget optimization

Allocate spend across cases using real capacity and value limits so the team maximizes the best opportunities.

W

Webhook processing

Verify Razorpay signatures, prevent duplicate processing, and build a clean event trail from payment changes to outcomes.

L

Ledger attribution

Track causality so the system knows which action created the recovery and which changes were simply natural recovery.

D

Dashboards

Give teams a clear portfolio view with risk, decisions, outcomes, and action value in one place.

Why this matters

Payment failures are not just customer issues — they are revenue opportunities. Recovery intelligence helps teams act earlier, with better choices, and with measurable impact.
